## New complete experiences

Four source-first, internal-only editorial lessons:

- **Switching who speaks for a study group** — 我, 我们 and 你们 as the speaker and addressed group reverse.
- **Identifying a speaker, teacher and shared identity** — 我是学生。, 他是老师。 and 也是 across changed roles.
- **Reconstructing a drink question versus a food statement** — 你好, 你喜欢喝茶吗？ and 这个苹果很好吃。; question particle 吗 versus declarative food quality.
- **Ownership, denial and additional identity** — 我的, 不是 and 也是 in a mistaken badge/role service-desk scenario.

Each expands the existing Phase 26 **nine** activities with three genuinely distinct, original-player compatible activities:

1. **Guided whole-source Hanzi reconstruction** through the unchanged `typed` contract. Learners must reconstruct the *complete attested original expression* (including punctuation); swapping in a credible but different attested expression triggers its specific authored misconception correction and source aid.
2. **Independent changed-role reading** through the original `reading` contract. A new context explicitly changes the speaker, question, listener, referent or affirmation status. The choices and Pinyin remain the three exact admitted originals, and wrong-source choices give source-specific reasons.
3. **Third independent ungraded dialogue** via original `free-production`, with criteria tied to a third changed situation. Learners compose new dialogue independently and self-assess speaker/role/turn distinctions. No grade, FSRS credit or native app progress event.
