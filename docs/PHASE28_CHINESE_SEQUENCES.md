## Four substantial new Chinese independent-practice lessons

1. **Reconstructing a new group orientation:** I / our group / the addressed group (我, 我们, 你们), changing which person speaks and which group is included.
2. **Sequencing a tea question and a fruit description:** visitor greeting 你好, tea-preference question 你喜欢喝茶吗？ and this-apple statement 这个苹果很好吃。. The question marker 吗 is not a description of a fruit.
3. **Ordering identity statements before a role correction:** first-person student statement 我是学生。, third-person teacher statement 他是老师。, separate denial expression 不是.
4. **Separating badge ownership, denial and shared identity:** 我的, 不是, 也是 remain different grammatical functions, not interchangeable English-style fillers.
